## 🎯 Purpose of Building BizTrend AI
*   To help local Indian businesses, freelancers, and consultants make sense of their data without needing technical expertise.
*   To offer an affordable, easy-to-use tool that turns raw sales, expense, or customer data into powerful insights and decisions.
*   To reduce manual work in business reporting, invoicing, and analysis through automation and AI.
*   To support the digital transformation of SMBs, enabling them to compete with larger players using better data intelligence.
*   To create a long-term, scalable startup that grows into a trusted Indian SaaS product, eventually expanding globally.
